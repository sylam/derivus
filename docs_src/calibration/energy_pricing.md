# Energy Pricing

Historical estimators for the forward-curve processes of the Theory page
[Energy Pricing](../theory/energy_pricing.md): the Clewlow-Strickland forward curve
(`ForwardPrice.<Name>,<tenor>` columns) and the two-factor commodity carry curve
(`ForwardRate.<Name>,<tau>` columns holding the average carry to maturity). The conventions every
estimator follows are on [Historical Calibration](general.md).
